## 2025-02-19 - Optimize streak calculation loop\n**Learning:** The study planner was doing an O(N^2) streak calculation by running `Array.prototype.find` on date iterations inside a 365-day `for` loop. Transforming arrays to a `Set` of formatted date strings (`toISOString().slice(0, 10)`) before the loop reduces lookup times to O(1), improving time complexity to O(N).\n**Action:** When computing consecutive day streaks or iterating over chronological bounds, pre-compute lookup tables or Sets for active days outside the loop instead of searching arrays on each iteration.
